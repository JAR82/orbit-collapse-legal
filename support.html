Common questions

I can't restore my Remove Ads purchase.
Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore Purchases" while signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

My progress didn't carry over to a new device.
Orbit Collapse's best score and streak are stored locally on-device — they aren't currently synced across devices. Game Center leaderboard scores do carry over with your Apple ID.
