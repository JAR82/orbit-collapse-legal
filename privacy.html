Privacy Policy

Orbit Collapse, by RossBoyGames — last updated October 2026

What we collect

Orbit Collapse has no account or sign-up — we don't collect your name, email, or any personal information directly. Your best score, daily streak, and settings are stored on your device. The only gameplay information that leaves your device is the analytics described under "Analytics" below.

Advertising

Orbit Collapse shows ads through Google AdMob to keep the game free. AdMob may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. Before any cross-app tracking, iOS will ask your permission via the App Tracking Transparency prompt — if you decline, you'll still see ads, just not personalized ones. See Google's Privacy Policy for how AdMob handles data.

Analytics

Orbit Collapse uses GameAnalytics, a third-party analytics service, to understand how the game is played so we can improve it. GameAnalytics receives:

  • A device identifier — the identifier for vendor (IDFV) that iOS assigns to apps from the same developer on your device. It is not your name or your email. If you allow tracking in Apple's prompt, GameAnalytics may also receive your advertising identifier (IDFA) with these events.
  • Gameplay and progression events — for example when you start a run, the level you reach, and your score when a run ends.
  • Basic device and app information — such as device type, iOS version, app version, and connection type. GameAnalytics may also derive your approximate country from your IP address.

This data is used only for analytics, to improve the game. We don't use it to target ads or share it with advertisers. See GameAnalytics' Privacy Policy for how they handle data.

Your choices

EU and UK. If you're in the EU or UK, the first time you open the game you'll see a consent form (provided by Google) asking whether we may use your data for ads and analytics. Analytics stays off until you consent, and stays off if you decline. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy Settings in the game (Settings, via the gear icon), which reopens the form.

Tracking on iOS. iOS will also ask whether the game may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's tracking prompt). You can allow or deny it there, and change it any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Game Center

Leaderboards are powered by Apple's Game Center, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Your Game Center identity and submitted scores are handled entirely by Apple — we never see your Apple ID or personal details.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads purchase is processed entirely through Apple's App Store — we never see or store your payment information.

Children

Orbit Collapse is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13.

Data we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't run analytics beyond what's described above.
